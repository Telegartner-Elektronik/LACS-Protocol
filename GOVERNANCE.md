# LACS Governance

LACS is an open specification, publicly developed and published under this
name since 2025-11. Anyone may implement it, free of charge and without
permission. This policy only concerns the name, so that everything called
LACS interoperates. LACS is not a registered trademark; the policy is a
request to everyone who uses the name, not a licence condition.

## Welcome

- Implementing LACS in any product or service, commercial or not.
- Using the name for conforming implementations (see *Conformance* in
  [LACS.md](LACS.md#conformance)), stating the version (e.g. "supports LACS 1.0").
- Forking this repository.

## Please do not

- Call an incompatible protocol variant or fork "LACS". Incompatible forks
  should use a different name.
- Imply endorsement or certification by the maintainer. There is no
  certification of LACS.
- Register "LACS" or a confusingly similar name as a trademark. The name is
  meant to stay free for everyone.

## Maintainer

Telegärtner Elektronik GmbH currently maintains this repository. It does not
register the name and does not charge fees for its use. Companies that
actively contribute are welcome to take part in maintaining LACS.

Questions: please open an issue.
